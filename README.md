# Family Care Clinic — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Family Care Clinic is a family-oriented general practice serving Singapore’s Bukit Batok community since 1989. It provides adult and paediatric care, health screening, skin care and other outpatient medical services.

The website helps patients explore available care and contact the clinic. The redesigned homepage prioritises a readable introduction, clear contact actions and a broader service overview.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. A More Readable Hero

The headline now sits on a solid cream background, separate from the photograph. Previously, white text overlapped bright image areas. The revised layout gives the main message a consistent, readable backdrop.

### 2. Clear Contact Actions

Prominent contact buttons and two persistent mobile buttons make phone and WhatsApp options easier to access. Previously, these details appeared as plain text further down the homepage.

WhatsApp is clearly identified as non-urgent appointment booking only, setting expectations before patients make contact.

### 3. A Broader Service Overview

Six service pathways include descriptions and links, arranged in three columns on desktop and two on mobile. Previously, four services were introduced mainly through headings and images. The expanded overview makes more care options visible and explains each pathway before patients explore further.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** The cream headline area separates text from photography.
- **Hierarchy:** Prominent buttons make contact options easier to identify.
- **Responsive layout:** Service pathways use three desktop columns and two mobile columns.
- **Consistency:** Service descriptions and links follow a clear format, while WhatsApp labels communicate its booking restriction.
- **Mobile experience:** Two persistent contact buttons remain accessible while browsing.
