How do I request an appointment?

Call 6567 1775 or send a non-urgent appointment request through WhatsApp on 8799 4157. Please wait for the clinic to confirm the appointment.

Does the clinic see children?

Yes. We provide paediatric acute and chronic care, childhood vaccination and developmental assessment.

Can I discuss a skin concern?

Yes. We provide care for acne, pigmentation, scars, hair loss and warts. Our doctors are trained in Family Medicine and have a special interest in Dermatology.

How should I prepare for screening?

Preparation depends on the package and tests. Contact the clinic for instructions, current pricing and appointment availability before your screening.

Are subsidies available?

We participate in Healthier SG, CHAS and MediSave under CDMP, among other schemes. Check the government schemes page and ask the clinic to confirm your eligibility and applicable coverage.
